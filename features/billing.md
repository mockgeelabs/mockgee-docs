# Billing and Plans

Mockgee SaaS uses a simple plan model: Free, Pro, and Enterprise.

## View Your Plan

Open **Administration -> Billing**.

The billing page shows:

- current plan
- usage against plan limits
- available plan options
- waitlist actions for paid plans while checkout is not enabled

## Plans

| Plan | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Free | Individual testing, demos, and small mock APIs. |
| Pro | Teams that need higher limits and collaboration. |
| Enterprise | Larger teams that need advanced governance, private deployment, SSO/RBAC, and priority support. |

## Current Billing Behavior

During early release, Mockgee does not process payments inside the product. Workspaces start on Free, and paid plans can use waitlist/contact flows until checkout is enabled.

Plan limits and feature gates are enforced by workspace entitlements.
